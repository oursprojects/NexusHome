Power Infrastructure

Dual-Source Power & Solar Backup Architecture

Designed with an automatic transfer module, 12V 7Ah sealed lead-acid storage, and solar replenishment to guarantee 24/7 autonomous home automation without cloud or internet reliance.

Dual-Input Power Supply

AC Mains + 20W Solar PV

The system operates continuously via a 12V 5A regulated adapter (60W max output) paired with a 20W monocrystalline solar panel feeding a 10A PWM solar charge controller for green, off-grid energy replenishment.

Automatic Backup & Battery

XH-M350 + 12V 7Ah SLA (84Wh)

An integrated XH-M350 automatic transfer module seamlessly switches to the 12V 7Ah sealed lead-acid battery (84Wh nominal) the instant AC power drops, keeping firmware and relays active with zero reboot interruption.

XH-M350 Switchover 12V 7Ah Battery 84 Wh Nominal Capacity

Dual-Rail Electrical Isolation

LM2596 Step-Down Regulator

High-current appliances (15W Fan, 7W Light, JGY-370 Motor) run on the direct 12V high-power rail, while an LM2596 high-efficiency buck converter isolates sensitive 5V/3.3V logic (ESP32, VC-02, OLED, sensors) from inductive noise.

LM2596 Buck Converter 12V High-Power Rail 5V/3.3V Logic Rail

Battery Safety Envelope & 3-Stage Voltage Guard

To maximize SLA battery lifespan and eliminate destructive deep discharge, the PWM controller enforces strict hardware thresholds:

13.7V

Float Charge Stage

Continuous trickle maintenance charging that preserves battery capacity at full saturation without boiling off electrolyte or generating internal heat.

12.6V

Load Reconnect Threshold

Once LVD triggers, loads remain safely isolated until the battery recharges to 12.6V. This hysteresis gap prevents harmful rapid on/off cycling caused by surface voltage bounce.

10.7V

Low-Voltage Disconnect (LVD)

Critical hardware cutoff point. When battery drops to 10.7V under load, output disconnects immediately to shield the SLA cell from irreversible sulfation and permanent damage.

Actuation & Memory

Zero-Sensor Motorized Curtain & Blackout Recovery Engine

The JGY-370 12V high-torque worm-gear motor (30 RPM) drives the physical curtain through a calibrated 25-second traverse without mechanical limit switches. The ESP32 tracks millisecond runtime in memory and checkpoints progress to NVS flash every 500 ms, enabling automated recovery if power is interrupted mid-transit.

Blackout Recovery State Flow

How the firmware guarantees curtain integrity across unexpected power disruptions:

1

Asynchronous Runtime

Non-blocking cooperative timers drive OPEN (GPIO18) and CLOSE (GPIO19) relays for 25,000 ms, animating OLED progress while servicing Bluetooth serial in parallel.

2

500ms NVS Checkpointing

Every 500 milliseconds during movement, current state and accumulated elapsed milliseconds are written to non-volatile Preferences flash storage.

3

Blackout Interruption

If power fails mid-transit (e.g. second 10 of a 25s closing stroke), the motor stops immediately. The saved 10-second elapsed position remains permanently preserved in flash.

4

Autonomous Resumption

Upon power restoration, the ESP32 reboots, detects an incomplete transit, calculates the remaining 15 seconds, and automatically drives the motor until 100% closed.

Edge Intelligence

Climate Automation & Offline Voice Control Rules

Automated closed-loop intelligence operating completely on the ESP32 and VC-02 hardware with zero internet dependency.

Automatic Climate Mode

Hysteresis Control Core

When Automatic Mode is active, the ESP32 samples the DHT22 every 2.5s. If temperature reaches ≥30°C, the fan turns ON accompanied by a 1.5-second buzzer notification. When temperature drops to ≤29°C, the fan turns OFF. The 1°C deadband prevents relay chattering.

≥30°C Fan ON ≤29°C Fan OFF 1.5s Buzzer Alert

Manual Mode Override

Direct User Actuation

Selecting Manual Mode disables temperature-triggered fan switching and clears automated overrides. When manual mode is initiated, the fan is initially turned OFF for safety. The user retains complete direct control over light, fan, and curtains via mobile app or offline voice commands.

Overrides Disabled Safety Auto-OFF Direct User Control

Local Edge Speech Engine

The AI-Thinker VC-02 module runs completely offline on dedicated neural-audio hardware. It continuously listens for the wake word "Nexus". Commands are transferred over UART at 9600 baud with an integrated 350ms repeat-command debounce guard to prevent multiple triggers.

Step-by-Step VC-02 Flashing Procedure

Step 1 • Tool Setup
Download and extract hummingbird-m-update-tool.zip onto your Windows computer.
Step 2 • Serial Wiring
Wire the VC-02 to a USB-to-UART adapter (CH340/CP2102): 3.3V/5V, GND, TX → RX, RX → TX.
Step 3 • Port & File Selection
Launch Hummingbird-M, select your COM port, and browse to select NexusHome/output/uni_app_release.bin.
Step 4 • Burn Firmware
Click Download / Flash. Wait for the progress bar to complete 100% and report successful flash verification.
