Capabilities

Engineered for Reliability & Everyday Comfort

NexusHome bridges physical micro-controllers with a native Android experience for zero-latency, private home management.

Real-Time Climate Tracking

High-accuracy DHT22 digital temperature sensing with automatic hysteresis: triggers fans ON at ≥30°C and restores OFF at ≤29°C.

25s Motorized Curtains

Timed bidirectional relay control with power-loss memory persistence (NVS) to ensure smooth full-traverse opening and closing cycles.

Offline Voice Commands

Built-in VC-02 speech module over UART for instant offline voice control: "lights on/off", "fan on/off", "open/close curtain", and "automatic/manual mode".

Bluetooth SPP Connectivity

Direct RFCOMM serial communication with automatic exponential-backoff reconnecting up to 5 attempts, keeping your connection stable.

Local Room DB Audit Log

Every single command and actuation is recorded locally using Android Room SQLite with timestamped history and clear-all protection.

SSD1309 2.42" OLED UI

The ESP32 microcontroller features a dedicated 20 FPS high-contrast OLED display with animated startup, live heartbeat, and device status cards.

Custom Hardware Engineering

The Physical NexusHome Hub

Controlled directly by the NexusHome Android app via offline Bluetooth Classic SPP. Engineered with battery backup, active thermal control, optocoupled relay isolation, and offline voice processing.

Exterior Enclosure

Front View & Status Display Enclosure

Precision custom wall-mounted chassis presenting the SSD1309 2.42" high-contrast OLED screen, system status indicators, and sleek matte finish.

12V 7Ah Battery & 20W Solar

Motolite sealed lead-acid battery (84Wh nominal) paired with a 20W monocrystalline solar panel, 10A PWM charge controller, and XH-M350 automatic backup switchover.

4-Channel Isolated Relays

Active-LOW drivers controlling 12V 7W lighting (GPIO2), 12V 15W cooling fan (GPIO15), and bidirectional JGY-370 12V 30RPM motorized curtains (GPIO18/19).

2.42" OLED & DHT22 Sensor

High-contrast SSD1309 physical dashboard running at 20 FPS via I²C (GPIO21/22) paired with a DHT22 sensor on GPIO33 sampling every 2.5s with 30°C ON / 29°C OFF hysteresis.

VC-02 Voice AI & Dual Fans

AI-Thinker VC-02 offline speech processor over UART (GPIO16/17 @ 9600 baud) with "Nexus" wake word and 350ms debounce, dual brushless cooling fans, and 5V active buzzer.

Built with Modern Engineering Standards

The mobile application uses Kotlin with Android Jetpack, implementing Model-View-ViewModel (MVVM) separation of concerns. The embedded firmware uses non-blocking cooperative scheduling (millis()) to drive 20 FPS OLED animations, reliable serial streaming, and motor safety cutoffs simultaneously.
