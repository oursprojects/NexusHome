Capabilities

Engineered for Reliability & Everyday Comfort

NexusHome bridges physical micro-controllers with a native Android experience for zero-latency, private home management.

Real-Time Climate Tracking

High-accuracy DHT22 digital temperature sensing with automatic hysteresis: triggers fans ON at ≥30°C and restores OFF at ≤29°C.

25s Motorized Curtains

Strictly time-based 25-second full-stroke traverse (full open and full close only, no partial stops) with 500ms NVS power-loss memory persistence.

Offline Voice Commands

AI-Thinker VC-02 offline neural DSP over UART. Wake once with "Nexus" and chain multiple commands freely without repeating the wake word; automatically enters standby after 10s of inactivity.

Bluetooth SPP Connectivity

Direct RFCOMM serial communication with automatic exponential-backoff reconnecting up to 5 attempts, keeping your connection stable.

Local Room DB Audit Log

Every single command and actuation is recorded locally using Android Room SQLite with timestamped history and clear-all protection.

SSD1309 2.42" OLED UI

The ESP32 microcontroller features a dedicated 20 FPS high-contrast OLED display with animated startup, live heartbeat, and device status cards.

4x2 Home Screen Widget

Live Android home screen controller with real-time temperature, climate comfort hints, and 4 one-tap toggles for light, fan, curtain, and automation.

Control Center Quick Tiles

Full 4-tile Quick Settings suite (Nexus Light, Fan, Curtain, Mode) accessible straight from Android's status shade with background persistence.

Synchronized Curtain Animation

Multi-stage parting vector animation with real-time dot indicators and thread-safe mutex serialization shared seamlessly across app, widget, and tiles.

Built with Modern Engineering Standards

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about NexusHome? Find everything you need to know about our 100% offline architecture, voice commands, motorized curtains, climate automation, and hardware setup.

No, you do not need to repeat "Nexus" before every command.

Saying the wake word "Nexus" once initiates an active listening session confirmed by a pleasant audio chime. From there:

  • Chain Commands Freely: While active, you can issue commands back-to-back without saying the wake word again (e.g. speak "lights on", then "fan on", then "open curtain").
  • 10-Second Inactivity Auto-Standby: If no command is received within 10 seconds, or if the acoustic model detects you are conversing with someone else, Nexus automatically announces "goodbye" and returns to low-power standby mode waiting for the wake word again.
  • Manual Sleep: You can also speak "goodbye" or "bye" anytime to return to standby immediately.

No. Curtain operation is strictly full open and full close only.

NexusHome uses a high-torque 12V 30RPM worm-gear motor (JGY-370) operating on a calibrated 25-second time-based stroke without mechanical limit switches. Control is binary: either Full Open or Full Close. Intermediate stopping or partial percentage positions are intentionally not supported to ensure reliable, zero-sensor end-to-end traversal every single time. Both the physical OLED dashboard and the mobile app render live filling/draining progress bars across the 25-second transit.

Curtain transit progress is never lost, thanks to non-volatile flash checkpoints.

Every 500 milliseconds during motorized travel, the ESP32 writes the current movement state and accumulated elapsed milliseconds into Preferences Non-Volatile Storage (NVS). If mains power is abruptly cut mid-motion (for instance, at second 11 of a 25-second closing cycle):

  1. The motor stops instantly when power drops.
  2. When power returns (or the Motolite 12V UPS battery takes over), the ESP32 boots up, reads the checkpoint from NVS, and recognizes an unfinished transit.
  3. It calculates the remaining time (e.g. 14 seconds) and automatically finishes the stroke until 100% completed, preventing mechanical overruns.

NexusHome features two dedicated operating modes tailored for either automated climate regulation or direct user command:

  • Automatic Mode (OLED displays AUTO): The DHT22 digital sensor samples ambient room temperature every 2.5 seconds. When temperature reaches ≥ 30.0°C, the cooling fan turns ON automatically accompanied by a 1.5s buzzer chime. When the room cools down to ≤ 29.0°C, the fan turns OFF. The 1.0°C hysteresis window prevents rapid relay clicking.
  • User Mode / Manual Override (OLED displays MANUAL): Triggered by saying "user mode", toggling in the app, or tapping the quick tile. Automatic temperature triggers are disabled, and the fan turns OFF initially for safety. You have 100% direct control over the light, fan (ON/OFF), and motorized curtains.

Yes! NexusHome includes two zero-launch Android control interfaces:

  • 4x2 Home Screen Widget: Pin the interactive widget to your Android launcher. It displays real-time ambient temperature, comfort status badges, and 4 one-tap toggle buttons for Lighting, Fan, Curtains, and Automation Mode.
  • Control Center Quick Settings Tiles: Swipe down your phone's notification shade from anywhere — even while inside full-screen games or streaming videos. Tap dedicated tiles for Nexus Light, Nexus Fan, Nexus Curtain, or Nexus Mode to send background Bluetooth commands instantly.

The official NexusHome Android APK supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) up to Android 16 (Baklava, API 36).

To respect your privacy, only permissions strictly necessary for Bluetooth hardware control are requested:

  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT & BLUETOOTH_SCAN: Required on Android 12+ to discover and maintain RFCOMM serial connections with the ESP32.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Required by older Android operating systems (Android 8 to 11) exclusively to scan for nearby Bluetooth peripherals. NexusHome never tracks or records your GPS location.
  • Zero Internet, Camera, or Storage Permissions: The app does not request or possess internet access.

The custom wall-mounted chassis houses a complete industrial-grade control hub:

  • Core Microcontroller: ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin) with 240MHz dual-core Xtensa LX6 CPU and onboard Bluetooth Classic.
  • Isolated Relays: 4-channel active-LOW relay board driving 12V 7W LED light (GPIO2), 12V 15W fan (GPIO15), and bidirectional JGY-370 worm-gear motor (GPIO18/19).
  • Sensors & Display: DHT22/AM2302 precision temperature sensor (GPIO33) and an SSD1309 2.42" 128x64 high-contrast I²C OLED screen (GPIO21/22) rendering a 20 FPS live dashboard.
  • Voice Module: AI-Thinker VC-02 neural audio processor on hardware UART (GPIO16/17 @ 9600 baud).
  • Power & Backup: Motolite 12V 7Ah UPS sealed lead-acid battery, LM2596 buck converters (stepping 12V down to stable 5V and 3.3V rails), and dual brushless side cooling fans for thermal exhaust.

Every actuation is stored strictly on your local phone via an embedded Android Room SQLite database.

Whenever you tap a toggle or send a voice command, a timestamped audit record is generated locally (e.g. "Fan command - Turned ON" with exact date and time). This data never syncs to any remote server. You can open the History screen in the app at any time to review device activity, or permanently erase every saved entry by tapping "Clear History".

Initial connection takes less than 30 seconds:

  1. Power ON the Hub: Ensure the NexusHome enclosure is connected to power. The 2.42" OLED screen will display the startup boot screen and initialize the dashboard.
  2. Pair in Android Settings: Open your smartphone's Settings → Bluetooth, select Pair new device, and choose "NexusHome" (pair with PIN 1234 or confirm prompt).
  3. Open App & Connect: Launch the NexusHome app, grant Bluetooth permissions if prompted, and tap Connect. The dashboard will instantly transition to Connected state and display live room temperature telemetry.
